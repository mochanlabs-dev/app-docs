A quick tour of the screens

Where to find things, in one minute.

#The top bar

Item What it does
☰ menu Opens the main menu: Weighment, Masters, Reports, Accounts, Administration.
Tally Sync Posts sales and purchases that are waiting to Tally right now. See Tally.
Govt Sync Sends weighments that are waiting to the government portal. See Government portal.
Your name Light or dark mode, Change Password, Logout.

#The main menu

Section What's inside
Weighment Token, Sale (Outward), Purchase (Inward), and Production if your site uses it.
Masters Vehicles, Parties, Materials, Party Rates, Locations, Divisions.
Reports Daily Log, Sale Reports, Purchase Reports, Client List, Vehicle History, Audit Log, MDTSS Sync Status.
Accounts Ledgers, Account Groups, Voucher Entry, Inventory, Shifting.
Administration Everything in Setup: users, hardware, printing, Tally, license and more.

You only see the screens your role allows.

#Dark mode

Open the menu under your name and choose Dark. A True black background switch appears for night shifts.

Tip

On the Token form and the billing dialogs, press Enter to save. You rarely need the mouse at the platform.

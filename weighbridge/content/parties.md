## Parties

Open **Masters → Parties**. A party is a customer or a supplier.

![Parties](/img/parties.png "The party master.")

Press **Add** and fill in the name, city, phone and, if the party has them, **GSTIN** and **PAN**. **Credit limit** records how much credit the party is allowed.

:::tip
Keep one entry for walk-in customers (for example *Cash*) and use it for cash sales.
:::

## Party rates

Open **Masters → Party Rates**, choose a party and set, for each material:

- **This party's rate**: the rate this party pays.
- **Deduction / Qtl**: an amount taken off that rate for every quintal.
- **Billed rate** (shown for you) = rate − deduction.

![Party rates](/img/party-rates.png "Rates for one party. Materials with nothing set are billed at the standard rate.")

Press **Save** on a row to keep it, or **Clear** to go back to the standard rate. When you [bill a sale](/sell/) for this party, rate and deduction fill in automatically.

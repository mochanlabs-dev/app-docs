title: Company details
category: Setup
order: 1
summary: The name, address and GSTIN printed on every slip and invoice.
---

Open **Administration → Company details**.

![Company details](/img/company.png "Company details shown on slips and invoices.")

| Field | Used on |
|---|---|
| **Legal name** | The heading of every weight slip, token and invoice. |
| **Address** | Under the name on the tax invoice. |
| **GSTIN** | Top left of the tax invoice. Also used for the Tally company when Tally needs it. |
| **Phone**, **Email** | Printed on the invoice. |
| **Logo URL** | Optional logo image. |

Press **Save**. New receipts use the change immediately. Receipts already printed stay as they were.

:::warn
Check the **legal name** and **GSTIN** carefully. They appear on tax invoices.
:::

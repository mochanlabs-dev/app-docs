## Automatic check

Each time Weighbridge starts, it checks for a new version. If there is one, it asks:

1. **Download it now?** Choose **Yes** to download. You can keep working while it downloads; a progress bar shows how far it is.
2. When the download is complete, the app asks to **restart**. Choose **Restart now**, or **Later**. If you pick *Later*, the update installs automatically the next time you close the app.

Your data is never touched by an update.

:::tip
Update at a quiet time, such as the start of the day or after the last vehicle, and take a [backup](/backups/) first.
:::

## Check yourself

Open **Administration → About** and press **Check for updates**. The same prompts follow. This only works in the installed Weighbridge app, not in a browser tab.

![About](/img/about.png "Version, license and the Check for updates button.")

## Which version do I have?

**About** shows the **Version** and when it was last updated.

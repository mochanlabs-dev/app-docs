Open the **Reports** menu. Every report has an **Export** button (Excel or PDF) and a date range or date picker. If a report looks empty, widen the dates first.

## Daily Log

Everything that happened on one day. Choose a **Date**, then switch between two tabs:

- **Weighment Log**: each completed weighing with direction (**Inward**/**Outward**), time, vehicle, material, party, **Gross**, **Tare** and **Net**.
- **Token Log**: every token, including ones still open.

![Daily Log](/img/daily-log.png "A day's weighments at a glance.")

## Sale Reports

Choose **From**, **To** and optionally a **Party**. Tabs: **Sale List**, **Product Wise**, **User Wise** and **Kaanta Charges**.

![Sale Reports](/img/sale-reports.png "Invoice, date, vehicle, material, party, weights and amount.")

## Purchase Reports

Choose **From** and **To**. Tabs: **Purchase List**, **Summary**, **Gate Wise**, **Division Wise**, **Vehicle Wise**, **Balance Report** and **Vehicle Summary**. The list shows net weight, amount, GST, net amount, gate, division, owner and PAN, with a **Total Net Amt**.

![Purchase Reports](/img/purchase-reports.png "Purchases with GST and net amounts.")

## Other reports

| Report | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Client List** | Your parties and their details. |
| **Vehicle History** | Every weighing for one vehicle. |
| **Audit Log** | Who did what and when: sign-ins, changes and manual-weight overrides. |
| **MDTSS Sync Status** | See [Sync status](/sync-status/). |

:::tip
Export before you change a master record you want to compare, for example parties or rates.
:::

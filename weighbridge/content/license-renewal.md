## Check your license

Open **Administration → License**.

![License](/img/license.png "Status, owner, expiry date, last check and this computer's ID.")

| Row | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Status** | **Active** means everything is fine. |
| **Licensed to** | The business the license was issued to. |
| **Expires** | The date, with the number of days left. |
| **Last verified** | The last time this computer checked in with the license server. |
| **This computer's ID** | A unique ID for this computer. Quote it if you contact Mochan Labs about the license. |

Press **Check now** to check immediately. The same details, with the **Valid until** date, are also under **About**.

## Expiry notice

In the last **30 days** before expiry, a notice appears every time someone signs in and again in **About**, telling you the date. It does not stop anyone working. It is a reminder to renew.

## Renew

1. Contact Mochan Labs ([support](/support/)) and renew the license.
2. Press **Check now** on the License page. The new expiry date appears. **There's nothing to reinstall and no key to type again.**

If a license expires and isn't renewed, the app returns to the [activation screen](/activate/) until it is.

## No internet?

The app keeps working for the grace period on your license (normally 7 days) without checking in. Connect the computer to the internet within that time.

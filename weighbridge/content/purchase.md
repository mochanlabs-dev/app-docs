A vehicle that arrives **loaded** and leaves **empty** is a purchase. After you exit it on the [Token page](/weigh/), it appears on **Weighment → Purchase (Inward)** under **Ready for billing**, with its **gross** and **tare** weights.

![The Purchase page](/img/purchase.png "Vehicles waiting to be billed on top, previous purchase receipts below.")

## Steps

1. Press **Complete billing** on the vehicle.
2. Choose the **Party**. Add **Gate Pass**, **Gate** and **Division** if you use them.
3. Enter the **Rate** (and **Less** if the party allows a deduction). The **Amount** is worked out for you.
4. If the material is subject to royalty, set **Royalty** to **Yes** and fill in the permit number (**M11**), the royalty weight and rate.
5. Save. The receipt is ready to print.

## What the receipt shows

The purchase receipt lists the weights and a **Billing** block: rate, amount, GST, income tax, transit fee, GST on transit and the **Net Amount**.

![A purchase receipt](/img/slip-purchase.png "Purchase receipt with the billing block.")

The percentages behind GST, income tax and transit fee are set once in [Workflow settings](/workflow/).

:::note
Some sites also record a *river weight* (the weight on the transit pass) next to the weighbridge weight. Turn on **Dual-location weighing** in [Workflow settings](/workflow/) to get that field.
:::

## Find an earlier purchase

**Previous receipts** lists the last 15. Search by purchase number or vehicle number and press **Receipt**.

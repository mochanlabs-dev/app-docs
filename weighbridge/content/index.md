## How a vehicle moves through Weighbridge

1. **Token (first weight).** The vehicle arrives on the platform and its first weight is recorded.
2. **Token exit (second weight).** The vehicle comes back across the scale. If it is **heavier** than on entry it is a **sale**; if it is **lighter** it is a **purchase**.
3. **Billing.** Complete the sale or purchase: party, rate, GST, charges. Weights always come from the scale; billing is the part you fill in.
4. **Receipt.** Print the weight slip and tax invoice.
5. **Sync.** Sales and purchases are posted to Tally and sent to the government portal automatically, and retried if either is offline.

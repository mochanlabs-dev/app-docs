Glossary

The terms used across the app and these pages.

Term Meaning
Token The record opened when a vehicle first arrives and is weighed. It stays open until the vehicle is exited.
Entry weight / first weight The weight recorded when the vehicle arrives.
Exit weight / second weight The weight recorded when it leaves.
Gross Vehicle plus load.
Tare The empty vehicle.
Net Gross minus tare: the material.
Sale (Outward) A vehicle loaded at your site (exit heavier than entry).
Purchase (Inward) A vehicle unloaded at your site (exit lighter than entry).
Qtl (quintal) 100 kg.
MT / t Metric tonne, 1000 kg.
Bill / Invoice number The token number, or the next number in your bill series.
Party A customer or supplier.
Party rate The rate a particular party pays for a material.
Kaanta The weighing charge.
Daala A loading/handling charge.
Frate Freight.
HSN The tax classification code printed on the invoice.
CGST / SGST / IGST The GST components on an invoice.
Royalty, M11, Form J Mining royalty and the transit permit paperwork some purchases carry.
MDTSS The Uttarakhand government weighbridge reporting portal.
Tally The accounting software Weighbridge posts vouchers to.
Override Entering a weight by hand, with a reason, when the scale can't supply one.
Dead-lettered A sync message that stopped retrying and needs a manual Retry.
Server / Client In a multi-computer site: the computer holding the data, and the ones that connect to it.

#Default roles

Role Typically used for
Administrator Everything, including users, setup and integrations.
Power Operator Day-to-day work plus supervisor actions.
Operator Weighing and billing at the platform.

Roles are fully editable in Users, roles and permissions. These are only starting points.
